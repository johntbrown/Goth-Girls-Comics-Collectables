From the Archives

Cable

Real name: Nathan Summers

Known for: X-Force, New Mutants, time travel, techno-organic virus, oversized future weapons.

Cable is the time-traveling son of Cyclops and Madelyne Pryor, a clone of Jean Grey. Infected as an infant with a techno-organic virus, Nathan was sent into the far future to survive. He grew into a battle-hardened mutant soldier and eventually returned to the present, where he became the mentor who transformed the New Mutants into X-Force.

His telepathy and telekinesis are enormously powerful, but much of that power is constantly devoted to holding the techno-organic virus in check.
