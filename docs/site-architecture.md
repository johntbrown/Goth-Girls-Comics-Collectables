# Site Architecture

## Primary navigation

### Home
Homepage focused on shopping, discovery, community, and physical-store traffic.

### Shop
Recommended collections:
- New Arrivals
- Comics
- Graphic Novels
- Manga
- Action Figures
- Anime
- Trading Cards
- Toys
- Statues & Collectibles
- Vintage & Rare
- Preorders
- Sale

### New This Week
A recurring merchandising destination for newly released comics, manga, toys, and collectibles.

### Preorders
Upcoming products with release information and preorder deadlines where relevant.

### Events
Store events including:
- Comic release events
- Creator appearances and signings
- Trading card tournaments
- Gaming events
- Community nights
- Sales events
- Collector events
- Holiday events

### Community
Long-term home for:
- Event recaps
- Community announcements
- Collector spotlights
- Staff recommendations
- Customer collections
- Local clubs
- Photos

### News & Announcements
Posts for:
- New arrivals
- Store announcements
- Event announcements
- New comic releases
- Preorder deadlines
- Sales and promotions
- Collectible drops
- Store updates

### About
Brand story, owner/store background, and community mission.

### Visit the Store
Address, hours, contact information, directions, local pickup information, and real store photography.

### Contact
Contact form, store contact information, social channels, and customer-service details.

# Homepage Wireframe

1. **Hero**
   - Strong comic-shop visual
   - Primary CTA: Shop New Arrivals
   - Secondary CTA: Visit the Store
   - Optional CTA: See What's New This Week

2. **Shop by Category**
   - New Comics
   - Back Issues
   - Graphic Novels
   - Manga
   - Action Figures
   - Anime
   - Trading Cards
   - Toys
   - Statues & Collectibles
   - Vintage & Rare

3. **New This Week**
   - Recently released comics, manga, toys, and collectibles

4. **Featured Collectibles**
   - Higher-value, limited, rare, or collector-focused merchandise

5. **Preorders**
   - Upcoming products and relevant deadlines

6. **Rare Finds / Collector Spotlight**
   - Vintage, graded, rare, or hard-to-find inventory

7. **Upcoming Events**
   - Next 3–4 events with date, title, type, and description

8. **Community**
   - Introduce the store as a local gathering place

9. **News & Announcements**
   - Recent store updates and product/news content

10. **Visit the Shop**
    - Store photo, address, hours, phone, directions, pickup information

11. **Newsletter / Drop Alerts**
    - First name + email
    - New arrivals, preorder deadlines, events, rare finds, special offers

12. **Footer**
    - Shop links
    - Events
    - Community
    - About
    - Contact
    - Store information
    - Social links
    - Newsletter
    - Shipping/returns/privacy/terms

## UX principles
- Product photography carries most of the visual weight
- Search is highly visible
- New Arrivals, New This Week, and Preorders are permanent navigation pillars
- Avoid excessive animation
- Mobile usability is a first-class requirement
- Shopping and product discovery come before decorative effects
