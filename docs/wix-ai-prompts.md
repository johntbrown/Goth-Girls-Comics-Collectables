# Wix AI Prompts

## Initial site generation prompt

Create a modern ecommerce website for Goth Girls Comics & Collectables, a local comic book and collectibles shop that sells comic books, graphic novels, manga, anime merchandise, action figures, toys, trading cards, statues, vintage collectibles, and pop-culture merchandise.

The site should feel like a real comic shop for serious collectors and fans, not a generic toy store. It should be visually grounded in comic-book subculture, with a darker, more mature edge inspired by goth aesthetics, vintage comic art, collector culture, and old-school comic shops.

The primary audience is men ages 36 to 55 who love comic books, anime, toys, action figures, trading cards, retro collectibles, and pop culture, while still being welcoming to all fans and collectors.

### Main goals
- Sell products online
- Highlight new arrivals and weekly comic releases
- Promote preorders and limited-edition items
- Promote store events and community activity
- Share store news and announcements
- Capture emails for newsletters, preorder alerts, and product drops
- Encourage visits to the physical store

### Pages and features
Include:
- Home
- Shop
- New This Week
- Preorders
- Events
- Community
- News & Announcements
- About
- Visit the Store
- Contact

Include ecommerce functionality such as:
- Product categories
- Product search
- Filters
- Cart and checkout
- Customer accounts
- Local pickup
- Shipping
- Promotional offers
- Newsletter signup forms

### Homepage content
The homepage should include:
- A strong hero section
- Featured products
- New comics this week
- Featured collectibles and toys
- Preorders
- Upcoming events
- Store announcements
- Newsletter signup
- Store hours, address, and contact details
- Social media links

### Design direction
The design should feel:
- Dark, bold, collector-focused, and subculture-driven
- Inspired by classic comic shops, vintage comic covers, underground comic energy, anime fandom, and collectible culture
- Stylish and memorable, with a slight goth edge
- Designed for adults, especially nostalgic collectors
- Professional and easy to shop

Use:
- Bold typography
- Dark backgrounds with selective accent colors
- Comic-panel-inspired layout touches
- Halftone textures
- Subtle grunge or print-style texture
- Vintage comic energy mixed with modern ecommerce usability

Preferred color direction:
- Black
- Charcoal
- Deep purple
- Crimson or dark red
- Off-white or cream
- Accent colors used carefully, not excessively

### Guardrails
- Do not make the site feel childish
- Do not make it overly cute or feminine
- Do not make it look like a superhero costume site
- Keep it clean, useful, and easy to navigate
- Product discovery and shopping usability should come first

The final result should feel like a serious comic and collectibles shop with personality, blending goth style, comic-book nostalgia, and modern ecommerce functionality.

## Newsletter form prompt

Add a Wix Forms signup for newsletters, new arrivals, preorder notifications, upcoming events, special offers, and limited collectible drops. Keep the signup simple, ideally first name and email address, with clear consent language. Include signup opportunities on the homepage, footer, preorder pages, and event/community areas.

## Store story prompt

Goth Girls Comics & Collectables was created from a love of comics, collectibles, pop culture, and the kind of local comic shops that feel like more than just a place to buy something.

The goal is to build a store where collectors, readers, gamers, families, and longtime fans can come in, discover something new, talk about what they love, and feel like part of a real community.

We want Goth Girls Comics & Collectables to have the personality and nostalgia of a classic neighborhood comic shop, while also giving customers a modern way to shop online, discover new releases, preorder upcoming items, and keep up with events and store news.

At its core, the idea is simple: create a fun, welcoming place for people who love comics and collectibles, whether they have been collecting for decades or are just getting started.
