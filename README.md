# Goth Girls Comics & Collectables

Planning repository for the Goth Girls Comics & Collectables ecommerce website.

## Project goal
Build a modern Wix-based ecommerce and community site for a local comic book and collectibles store. The site should sell products online, support product discovery and preorders, promote events and announcements, and strengthen the physical-store community.

## Primary audience
Adult comic and collectibles fans, especially men roughly 36–55 who are into comics, anime, action figures, toys, trading cards, retro collectibles, and pop culture, while remaining welcoming to all customers.

## Brand direction
- Serious modern comic and collectibles store
- Vintage comic / print culture influence
- Subtle goth / alternative personality
- Dark, bold, nostalgic, collector-focused
- Modern ecommerce usability first

Working ratio:
- 70% modern comic & collectibles retail
- 20% vintage comic / print culture
- 10% goth / alternative personality

## Core site areas
- Home
- Shop
- New This Week
- Preorders
- Events
- Community
- News & Announcements
- About
- Visit the Store
- Contact
